> There is a wall waiting for your work.
>
> From 10 to 25 October, BE Art Gallery hosts **I Luoghi dell'Anima**, a group
> exhibition open to every artist and every technique: painting, sculpture,
> mixed media, photography, art prints.
>
> The works inhabit three levels of a historic palazzo in the centre of
> Pistoia — stone, brick, vaulted ceilings — and draw their strength from that
> contrast.
>
> How to take part, terms and the form: **beartgallery.eu**
>
> BE Art Gallery & Creative Lab — Via San Pietro 24, Pistoia
